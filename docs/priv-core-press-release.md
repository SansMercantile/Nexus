# Press Release

**Sans Mercantile launches Priv Core, an autonomous trading desk, in South Africa**

*Issued by Swell PR & Communications*

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE:** 19 October 2026, 10:00 AM

*19 October 2026 | Cape Town, South Africa*

Sans Mercantile today launched Priv Core, an autonomous trading desk and AI trading terminal built for traders who want to test their strategies and keep control of their own funds. Priv Core is available in South Africa from today, with a free tier that needs no card to start.

## Priv Core combines

- Strategy backtesting on real candles
- 24/7 market watch across indices, synthetics, commodities and shares
- Seven timeframes from one minute to one day
- Full custody control — funds never leave the user's own broker

Priv Core is available on web, Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS, with one account across all six. To date, Priv Core has completed 152 backtest runs across more than 45 strategy boards and 7 timeframes.

> "Traders should be able to test an idea before they trust it, and keep control of their own funds. That is what Priv Core is built to do."
> \
> — **Mezzoforte Privilege Khoza, CEO, Sans Mercantile**

## Availability and pricing

Priv Core can be started free at [priv.sansmercantile.com](https://priv.sansmercantile.com), with no card required. Paid plans are also available. Details are at priv.sansmercantile.com.
