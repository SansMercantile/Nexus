CrazyJam DAW Extension — Installation Notice

By installing this extension you agree to the following:

1. The CrazyJam Bridge runs locally on your machine and communicates with the
   CrazyJam cloud backend for AI co-production features.

2. The extension installs FL Studio Piano Roll scripts, a Cubase MIDI Remote
   driver, and a Pro Tools HUI surface bridge, plus a startup monitor that
   launches the bridge automatically when your DAW opens.

3. No credentials are stored in the DAW scripts; the bridge holds your
   CrazyJam token locally.

4. This software is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind.
